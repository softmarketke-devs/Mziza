## Core Purpose

Mzazi Coach converts Kenyan CBC (Competency-Based Curriculum) report cards into actionable, plain-language guidance in Kiswahili and English. It includes home activities using everyday household materials, works offline by default, and provides feature phone access via USSD (`*384*77#`).
